# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises. Roles describe areas of accountability; one person may hold multiple roles, and some roles may be omitted or combined depending on project size and context. Teams should explicitly assign ownership for the responsibilities that apply to their project.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Project Sponsor champions the business case, provides organizational support, and is accountable for strategic alignment and high-level project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm that the initiative supports organizational goals and has an appropriate sponsor
- Help secure resources and resolve organizational impediments
- Make or escalate major scope, funding, and priority decisions outside the delivery team's authority
- Review progress against intended outcomes and address significant business-impacting risks

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with the Project Manager on governance, resource constraints, and escalations
- Aligns with the Product Manager on desired outcomes and priority trade-offs
- Receives status and risk updates from the Project Manager and provides timely decisions
- Consults stakeholders when decisions affect their interests

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
The Business Analyst helps clarify stakeholder needs, business processes, and requirements so the team can make work actionable and verifiable.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business and user needs
- Map relevant workflows and identify gaps or assumptions
- Help translate needs into backlog items, requirements, and acceptance criteria
- Maintain traceability between needs, decisions, and delivered outcomes where useful

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with the Product Manager to refine requirements and backlog priorities
- Engages stakeholders to validate needs and resolve ambiguity
- Partners with Developers and QA/Testing to clarify expected behavior and testable acceptance criteria
- Shares scope questions and dependencies with the Project Manager for planning and tracking

---

## UX/Product Designer

### Role Summary
The UX/Product Designer researches user needs and designs usable, accessible workflows and interfaces that support the product goals.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct or synthesize user research and identify usability needs
- Create and validate user flows, prototypes, and interface designs
- Communicate design intent, interaction details, and accessibility considerations
- Gather feedback and iterate on designs based on evidence

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with the Product Manager to connect user needs and design decisions to product outcomes
- Collaborates with Developers and the Technical Lead/Architect on feasibility and implementation details
- Works with QA/Testing to include usability and accessibility considerations in validation
- Shares research findings with stakeholders and coordinates design activities and dependencies with the Project Manager

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead/Architect guides technical direction and helps ensure the solution is maintainable, secure, and compatible with relevant systems and standards.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture, technical design, and integration decisions
- Identify technical risks and constraints and recommend mitigations
- Support estimation, technical breakdown, and engineering standards
- Promote testability, security, reliability, and maintainability in implementation decisions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinates with the Project Manager on technical dependencies, risks, and delivery sequencing
- Discusses feasibility and technical trade-offs with the Product Manager
- Guides Developers through design and implementation and collaborates on code reviews
- Works with QA/Testing to ensure designs are testable and quality risks are addressed
- Consults the Project Sponsor when significant technical choices affect business risk or scope

---

## DevOps/Release Engineer

### Role Summary
The DevOps/Release Engineer supports reliable build, deployment, and operational practices across delivery and release.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain or support CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Help ensure environments, configuration, and release procedures are ready
- Advise on observability, operational readiness, and rollback or mitigation plans
- Coordinate deployment verification and communicate operational risks

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers and the Technical Lead/Architect on automation, infrastructure, and system reliability
- Coordinates with QA/Testing on staging environments and release validation
- Works with the Project Manager on release timing, dependencies, and risk communication
- Aligns with the Product Manager on release scope and operational considerations that affect outcomes

---

## Customer Support/Operations Representative

### Role Summary
The Customer Support/Operations Representative brings customer-facing and operational insight into planning, release readiness, and post-release response.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring customer issues, support trends, and operational needs
- Help assess support readiness, documentation, and training needs before release
- Coordinate customer-impacting issue intake and provide relevant context during triage
- Communicate known issues and support guidance through agreed channels

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Shares customer feedback with the Product Manager to inform prioritization and product decisions
- Coordinates readiness, support materials, and stakeholder communications with the Project Manager
- Works with Developers and QA/Testing to provide reproducible issue details and validate fixes
- Collaborates with the DevOps/Release Engineer on operational signals and incident response where applicable

---

## QA/Testing

### Role Summary
QA/Testing validates that delivered work meets acceptance criteria and quality expectations. The responsibilities may be performed by a dedicated QA role or shared across the team, but ownership for validation should be clear.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and perform appropriate unit, integration, end-to-end, and manual testing
- Verify acceptance criteria and document defects or quality risks
- Support release smoke testing and communicate readiness findings
- Collaborate early on testability and prevention of defects

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with the Product Manager and Business Analyst to clarify expected behavior and acceptance criteria
- Partners with Developers and the Technical Lead/Architect on test design, defect triage, and resolution
- Coordinates release validation with the DevOps/Release Engineer and Project Manager
- Shares customer-impacting quality issues with the Customer Support/Operations Representative as appropriate

---

## Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Stakeholders provide relevant expertise, requirements, feedback, and decisions to help ensure project outcomes meet business and customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Share goals, constraints, and domain knowledge
- Review relevant plans, deliverables, and outcomes
- Provide timely feedback and decisions within their area of responsibility
- Raise risks or changes that may affect scope, priority, or adoption

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to validate needs, priorities, and outcomes
- Coordinate with the Project Manager for status, review schedules, dependencies, and escalations
- Engage with the Business Analyst and UX/Product Designer to clarify needs and validate proposed workflows
- Provide feedback to Developers and QA/Testing through agreed review and acceptance processes

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Assign roles based on project context; combine or omit roles where appropriate, while keeping accountability and handoffs explicit.
